Fan-made multi-season planner. Cyan fields are yours to edit; gray fields are calculated. Primostars are cumulative across seasons. The selected season defines a scoring baseline, not a ceiling (S1 Lv100/+10, S2 Lv130/+14, S3 Lv160/+17, then +30/+3); progress above that baseline continues to score. Relic planned levels are automatically limited by projected character level. Realm yields are calculated from the extracted client rules for the selected season rank, including object weights, damage, crits and break rewards. Daily refresh systems are counted only at actual reset events, with 5 Realm entries per refresh.
