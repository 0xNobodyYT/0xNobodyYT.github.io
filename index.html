Built for real decisions

Less guessing, less tab hopping

The goal is not another wiki. It is a practical layer over the game’s systems, focused on questions players actually need answered.

Data with context

Extracted configuration is paired with clear labels, assumptions and live-player checks where the client alone does not tell the whole story.

One connected suite

Every tool follows the same interface and links directly to the rest, so planning, comparing and browsing feel like one site.

Your data stays yours

Planner settings and saved loadouts use browser-local storage. Nothing requires an account today.

The next chapter

From tool collection to player platform

The free toolkit will keep growing. A future account layer could add cross-device saves, ad-free use and community build discovery when the project moves to its own domain.
